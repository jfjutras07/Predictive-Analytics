# Predictive Analytics

This repository contains predictive and advanced analytics projects across multiple domains, including workforce analytics, healthcare, education, energy efficiency, customer analytics, international development, and macroeconomic analysis.

## Analytical Projects Portfolio

| Project | Analytical Focus | Domain Context | Key Analytical Contributions |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Customer Segmentation and Analytics](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/Customer%20Analytics) | Behavioral segmentation and customer profile discovery | Marketing & customer analytics | Unsupervised learning framework combining feature engineering, clustering model comparison, customer archetype identification, and translation of behavioral segments into strategic marketing opportunities |
| [Employee Attrition Prediction & Behavioral Analysis](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/Employee%20Attrition) | Workforce retention risk prediction and organizational driver analysis | Human resources & workforce analytics | Classification modeling, behavioral clustering, engineered workforce indicators, model interpretation, and identification of structural and systemic factors influencing employee attrition |
| [Building Energy Efficiency Prediction](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/IBM_Supervised_ML_Project_Classification) | Prediction of building energy performance from architectural characteristics | Sustainable buildings & energy analytics | Regression modeling, model benchmarking, feature importance analysis, and identification of architectural factors influencing heating and cooling requirements |
| [Mental Health in Tech: Predicting Treatment-Seeking Behavior](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/IBM_Deep_Learning_Project) | Prediction and analysis of workplace factors influencing mental health support decisions | Organizational health & employee analytics | Classification modeling, statistical assessment, qualitative text analysis, topic modeling, and identification of organizational drivers affecting treatment-seeking behavior |
| [Country Development Clustering: Identifying Priority Nations for Aid](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/IBM_Unsupervised_ML_Project) | Socio-economic segmentation and development profile identification | International development & humanitarian analytics | PCA dimensionality reduction, clustering analysis, country profile assessment, and development-based prioritization framework for aid planning |
| [Macroeconomic Time Series Analysis – Central America](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/Macroeconomic%20Time%20Series%20Analysis) | Economic growth dynamics and short-term forecasting assessment | Macroeconomic & regional analytics | Panel time series analysis, stationarity testing, autocorrelation diagnostics, forecasting model evaluation, and assessment of short-term economic predictability |
| [Student Academic Performance & Resilience](https://github.com/jfjutras07/Predictive-Analytics/tree/main/Student%20Performance) | Academic performance prediction and educational risk assessment | Education analytics | Regression modeling, feature engineering, sensitivity analysis, predictive risk profiling, and development of intervention frameworks for student support |

## Organization

Each project is contained in its own folder at the root of this repository.

## Analytical Toolkit

Predictive modeling | Regression analysis | Classification modeling | Clustering analysis | Dimensionality reduction | Time series analysis | Feature engineering | Statistical analysis | Model interpretation | Decision-support frameworks

## Notes on Project Maturity

This repository serves both as a portfolio and as a continuous development environment. Projects demonstrate different analytical approaches depending on the decision context, ranging from predictive risk assessment and forecasting to segmentation and advanced statistical modeling. Some projects are intentionally scoped to explore specific analytical techniques, modeling strategies, or domain-specific decision frameworks. Insights and improvements from these projects are progressively consolidated into a shared analytical methodology used across more comprehensive end-to-end analytics projects.
